# Field visit quick guide — DEMO ONLY

This fictional guide demonstrates Fieldmate. It is not a real employer's manual, technical procedure, or safety instruction. Replace it with your own approved documents before field use.

## Before a visit

Before a field visit, check the site name, appointment time, contact person, and purpose of the visit. Download the relevant documents while you have internet access. Make sure the laptop is charged and you have a way to record your observations.

## Recording an observation

For each observation, record the site name, visit date, asset identifier when available, what you observed, and any follow-up needed. Separate what you actually observed from assumptions. If an identifier is unreadable, record it as unreadable instead of guessing.

## Naming photographs

Name a site photograph using the site name, visit date in YYYY-MM-DD format, and a short subject. For example: North-site_2026-10-02_entry-sign.jpg. Include an asset identifier in the filename when it is known.

## Missing information

If a site record is missing information, note the missing field and add a task to obtain it from the contact person. Do not invent an asset identifier, contact number, measurement, or completed action.

## Visit handover

A visit handover should include the site name, date, purpose of the visit, observations, unresolved questions, and open tasks. Include who owns each follow-up and the agreed due date if one was given. Mark the handover as a draft until you have checked it.

## Follow-up tasks

A follow-up task should say what needs to happen and identify the relevant site. Set a due date only when one has been agreed. Completing a task means the action has actually been done, not just mentioned in a note.

## Offline preparation

Before leaving internet coverage, open Fieldmate, check that both local models and the voice models are ready, and import the documents you need. Try one document question and one spoken note. Keep a separate backup of important records.
